## Core draft for editorial review

### About the service

D20 provides access to tabletop and board-game experiences in a browser. Available games and play options are shown in the catalog and on individual game pages.

### Accounts

Keep access to your account secure. Do not share passwords, one-time sign-in links, or provider access tokens. Contact support if you believe someone else has accessed your account.

### Acceptable use

Do not attempt to access another person's account, bypass access controls, or interfere with the operation of the service.

### Account closure

You can find the supported account and data-deletion process in Help. Contact support if you cannot access your account.

### Original candidate clauses for review

#### Current access and future paid offers

D20 is a personal project operated by its creator. Access is currently offered without a paid-access plan while the project develops. Paid access or other paid features may be offered in the future. If an offer is introduced, its price, included features and payment conditions will be presented before you choose to buy it. Continuing to use the current service does not by itself start a paid subscription.

Editorial status: the exact operator identity/contact and member state must be supplied in the operator section. The internal target of approximately 15-20 games is not a commitment to users. Do not advertise an existing subscription, imply that payment is required now, or guarantee that every future feature will remain free.

#### Possible voluntary support

Voluntary project support is compatible with the proposed product direction, but no contribution service or destination has been selected. Keep this as an internal option, with no live payment link or statement that contributions are already accepted.

If the operator chooses support without benefits, a candidate description is: `Supporting D20 is optional. A contribution does not buy game access, a subscription, ownership in the project, or a promise to deliver a particular game.` Use it only after confirming that the actual offer has no such benefits. Describe any recurring charge clearly and require a deliberate choice. Do not add an unconditional no-refunds clause or promise charitable/tax treatment. Country-specific registration, accounting, tax and payment-provider requirements remain to be reviewed.

If a contribution grants game access or another paid benefit, describe the actual offer and applicable payment, cancellation and consumer rights rather than relying on a donation label. Review price and offer information against the [EU contract-information guidance](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/consumers/shopping/contract-information/index_en.htm) before launching paid features. A future payment implementation has its own acceptance boundary; this content preparation adds no billing feature.

#### Permitted use and account security

Use D20's available features in accordance with these terms and the rules shown for the game you are playing. Protect access to your account and keep your sign-in methods secure. Do not impersonate another person, obtain access to another account, or disclose someone else's private information.

#### Fair play and service protection

Do not exploit a fault to gain an unfair advantage or interfere with another player's game. Do not introduce malicious code, bypass access controls, or deliberately disrupt the service. Use the contact page to report suspected abuse or a technical problem; do not include credentials or other players' private data in public reports.

Editorial decision: D20 publishes developer integration contracts. Any future rule about bots, automation, scraping or reverse engineering must distinguish abusive conduct from authorized development and rights that applicable law preserves. Do not copy a blanket prohibition that contradicts `/developers` or applicable software licenses.

#### Game materials and rights concerns

Access to a game through D20 does not give you permission to copy, redistribute or commercially exploit its artwork, rulebook or other protected materials beyond the permissions or legal exceptions that apply to you. If you believe material on D20 affects your rights, use the rights-holder contact page and identify the material, its location, your relationship to the rights and the concern you want reviewed.

Editorial decision: document the actual rights basis for each game's code, artwork, rules and branding. A fan-project description, attribution, free access or an offer to remove material is not evidence of permission. Do not assert publisher endorsement, comprehensive licensing or a statutory safe-harbor process without supporting facts. [WIPO's copyright FAQ](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/copyright/faq-copyright) explains that online availability does not itself authorize reuse and that exceptions depend on applicable law.

#### Availability

Game availability and features may change, and access can be interrupted by maintenance, technical faults or dependencies outside D20's control. Do not rely on a particular game, uninterrupted access or indefinite preservation of a game session. Nothing in this section excludes rights or remedies that applicable law does not allow us to exclude.

Editorial decision: confirm the service model and any actual availability commitments. This clause does not justify removing a paid entitlement without the notice, remedy or refund required by the applicable contract and law.

#### Restrictions, closure and complaints

Before publication, the operator must approve the grounds and practical procedure for restricting an account, protecting the service in an urgent incident, communicating a reason where appropriate, and reviewing a complaint. The published wording must match a procedure the operator can perform. Account suspension must not be described as personal-data deletion. Preserve the separate verified deletion instructions at `/help#delete-account` and the private support route.

#### Responsibility and changes

Preserve all liability and consumer rights that applicable law makes mandatory. Decide any additional limitation, governing-law clause, dispute venue and material-change notice only after the operator's country, audience and commercial model are known. Do not insert an arbitrary monetary cap, mandatory arbitration, unrestricted indemnity or a claim that D20 has no responsibility for anything.

Where EU consumer rules apply, unfair standard terms can be non-binding; a broad disclaimer does not automatically protect the operator. See [Your Europe on unfair contract terms](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/consumers/unfair-treatment/unfair-contract-terms/index_en.htm). The final wording and acceptance/notice mechanism need review by a lawyer familiar with the applicable jurisdiction. Merely adding a footer link does not establish that an agreement was validly accepted.
