D20 provides access to tabletop and board-game experiences in a browser. Available games and play options are shown in the catalog and on individual game pages.

Do not attempt to access another person's account, bypass access controls, or interfere with the operation of the service.

### Account closure

You can find the supported account and data-deletion process in Help. Contact support if you cannot access your account.
