## Legal guardrails for adapting the references

- **Establish applicability before selecting legal language.** GDPR Article 3 considers establishment and relevant offering/monitoring activities in the Union, not merely whether a service charges money. The operator has since confirmed individual operation from the EU with worldwide availability; exact member state and identity remain pending. Use the [launch-input record](README.md#operator-and-launch-inputs---2026-09-08), not the developer's timezone. [GDPR, Article 3](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679).
- **Describe actual processing.** If GDPR applies, identify the controller and contacts, purposes and legal bases, relevant data, recipients/transfers, retention periods or criteria, rights and complaint routes. Address indirect collection, such as identity-provider data, as applicable. The privacy notice must explain the processing rather than manufacture permission for unrecorded activities. [EDPB privacy-statement guidance](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/find-practical-info/faq_en), [Commission obligations](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/information-business-and-organisations/obligations_en).
- **Choose a basis per purpose.** Accepting Terms or acknowledging Privacy does not turn all processing into valid consent. Consent has specific requirements; a contract basis requires the relevant necessity. [EDPB lawful-processing guidance](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/be-compliant/process-personal-data-lawfully_en).
- **Make operational promises executable.** Retention needs a purpose-specific policy and deletion procedure. Rights requests need a monitored contact and response process; GDPR generally requires a response within one month, with applicable exceptions and extension rules. Do not promise immediate erasure of logs, backups or shared records without evidence. [EDPB data-protection basics](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/learn-the-basics/data-protection-basics_en), [EDPB individuals' rights](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/be-compliant/respect-individuals-rights_en).
- **Do not copy an age threshold.** GDPR Article 8 concerns consent-based processing for services offered directly to children: its default threshold is 16, with national reductions permitted to 13. Contractual capacity and other child-protection rules are separate questions. Decide eligibility and any parental process for the actual service and applicable law. [GDPR, Article 8](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679).
- **Review storage and providers separately.** Map cookies, local storage, external embeds, hosting, email, authentication and monitoring to actual purposes and recipients. Under applicable ePrivacy rules, terminal storage/access generally needs consent unless a relevant transmission or strictly necessary service exception applies. Privacy prose does not implement consent controls. GDPR processor contracts and transfer safeguards also require operational evidence. [Directive 2009/136/EC, Article 2(5), amending ePrivacy Article 5(3)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX%3A32009L0136), [EDPB controller/processor guidance](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/learn-the-basics/data-controller-or-data-processor_en).
- **Use bounded service limitations.** EU consumer terms must be understandable and fair; excessive liability exclusions and restrictions on legal remedies may be unenforceable. Free access is not by itself a universal consumer-law exemption: digital-service coverage can include personal-data arrangements, subject to statutory scope and exceptions. Preserve mandatory rights and obtain jurisdiction-specific review before adding liability caps, indemnities, venue, arbitration or unilateral-change clauses. [Your Europe unfair terms, checked 2026-08-05](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/consumers/unfair-treatment/unfair-contract-terms/index_en.htm), [Directive (EU) 2019/770, Article 3](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/en/ALL/?uri=oj%3AJOL_2019_136_R_0001).
- **Separate permission from disclaimers.** Copyrighted material generally needs authorization or a valid license/exception. An inference for D20: fan status, free access, attribution and a takedown email do not themselves establish permission to distribute game artwork, rulebooks or adaptations. Keep asset provenance and rights permissions independently verifiable; do not claim blanket publisher approval. [WIPO copyright FAQ](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/copyright/faq-copyright).
- **Do not assume a universal hosting exemption.** DSA duties vary by service role, size and activity. Where relevant, assess reporting, explanations and review processes before promising them in Terms; small size reduces some duties but is not a blanket exemption. This note does not classify D20 under the DSA. [Commission DSA questions and answers](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/digital-services-act-questions-and-answers), [Commission moderation-transparency guidance, updated 2026-09-07](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/dsa-brings-transparency).

## D20 drafting decisions

These are proposed editorial decisions, not findings that the corresponding behavior exists:

1. Help explains the verified journey from selecting a game to creating/joining a table, supported account/guest behavior, disconnections, game-specific rules and troubleshooting. Keep `#faq` and `#delete-account`; supply deletion steps and consequences only after #247 verifies them.
2. Privacy begins with a readable overview and operator/contact information, followed by an auditable data-purpose-basis-retention table, public visibility, recipients/transfers, browser storage, rights/deletion, children, security and updates. Fill each statement from the production data inventory and operator decisions owned by #248.
3. Terms covers service scope and eligibility, accounts, lawful use/fair play, intellectual property and permitted access, actual third-party dependencies, reporting, proportionate restrictions and review, availability/changes, termination/deletion, mandatory-rights-preserving liability language, contact and approved governing-law language.
4. Keep operational inputs visible in the internal drafts. Do not publish unresolved operator details, fictional retention periods, unsupported security/compliance guarantees, a non-working deletion process, or a claim that the documents eliminate legal risk.

Recheck legal applicability, source freshness and working public destinations before publication. This research is complete as source preparation; #247, #248 and the page delivery requirements remain separate completion gates.
