## Draft copy

### About D20

D20 brings tabletop and board games to your browser. Explore the catalog, open a game's page, and use its available play options to start a session.

### Find a game

Game pages bring together the available description, player information, and play options. Some catalog entries are still in development, so being listed does not always mean a game is ready to play.

Link: `Explore games` -> `/games`.

### Keep your games close

The D20 workspace keeps your open game sessions within reach while you browse the site. Each game provides its own play experience and rules.

### Build for D20

Developers can use D20's game specifications to understand how a compatible game client connects to a session and exchanges game actions and updates.

Link: `For developers` -> `/developers`.

### Need a hand?

Visit Help for getting started, account questions, and support.

Link: `How to play and FAQ` -> `/help`.
